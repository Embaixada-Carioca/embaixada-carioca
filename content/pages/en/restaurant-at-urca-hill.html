Quick answer

Is there a restaurant on Urca Hill?

Yes: Embaixada Carioca, at the first cable-car stop, inside Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park. It is the only restaurant in the park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain, operating since 2010.

It serves à la carte breakfast, Brazilian lunch with the Academia da Cachaça feijoada (through a formal partnership), snacks, sandwiches, caipirinhas and Heineken draft beer, every day from 8:30 AM to 9:00 PM.
