Quick answer

How does the Sugarloaf cable car work?

Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park connects Praia Vermelha to Urca Hill (220 m) and Sugarloaf Mountain (396 m) in two cable-car legs. The entrance is at Av. Pasteur, 520, in Urca, and tickets are sold on the official website and at the ticket offices.

At the first stop, Embaixada Carioca serves breakfast, lunch and drinks with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain. A restaurant reservation is separate from the park ticket.

The park

Much more than a cable car

Opened in 1912, the Sugarloaf cable car was the first in Brazil. Today, the cabins take visitors from Praia Vermelha to Urca Hill and, from there, to the top of Sugarloaf Mountain.

Urca Hill has viewpoints, the end of the trail and Embaixada Carioca. Sugarloaf Mountain offers a 360-degree panorama of Rio. Allow 3 to 5 hours for the full visit with a meal.

Step by step

A stop at Embaixada Carioca during your visit
