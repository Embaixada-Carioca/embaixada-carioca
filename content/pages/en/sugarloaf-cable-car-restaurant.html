Quick answer

Is there a restaurant on the Sugarloaf cable car?

Yes. Embaixada Carioca is at the first cable-car stop, on Urca Hill, before the leg up to Sugarloaf Mountain. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

It serves breakfast, Brazilian lunch with the Academia da Cachaça feijoada (through a formal partnership), caipirinhas and Heineken draft beer. The restaurant reservation and the park ticket are separate.

Why stop here

When to choose Embaixada Carioca

  • When you want to eat within the cable-car circuit, without going down to the neighborhood.
  • When the view of Sugarloaf Mountain is part of the experience.
  • When you want breakfast, Brazilian lunch, a caipirinha, draft beer or an event on Urca Hill.
