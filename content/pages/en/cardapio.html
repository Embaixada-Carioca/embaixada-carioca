Quick answer

What is on the menu?

Brazilian food from breakfast to sunset, on Urca Hill. Highlights are Brazilian-style picanha, Academia da Cachaça's feijoada (served through a formal partnership), snacks to share, caipirinhas and Heineken draft beer, considered the 2nd best in Brazil.
