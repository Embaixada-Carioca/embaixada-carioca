Quick answer

How to get to the restaurant on Urca Hill?

Go to Praia Vermelha, in Urca, and enter Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park at Av. Pasteur, 520. The first cable-car leg ends on Urca Hill, where Embaixada Carioca is. By cable car, you need a park ticket.

The free alternative is the Urca Hill trail, which starts at Pista Cláudio Coutinho, next to Praia Vermelha, when it is open. The climb takes about 30 to 40 minutes.

Going up

Two ways up to Urca Hill

By cable car

The fastest and most traditional way. The first leg links Praia Vermelha to Urca Hill. You need a Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park ticket; we recommend buying it in advance on the official website.

bondinho.com.br

Via the trail

Free when open, from Pista Cláudio Coutinho, in about 30 to 40 minutes. Before you go, check MONA's notices and plan your way down, because trail and restaurant hours differ.

To Praia Vermelha

How to get to Urca

Bus routes and times change: check your transport app on the day of your visit.

Taxi, Uber or 99

The most practical option. Use "Parque Bondinho Pão de Açúcar" or "Avenida Pasteur, 520". You are dropped near the ticket office.

Metro and bus

The most used station is Botafogo. From there, take a taxi, ride-hailing app or bus to Praia Vermelha; line 513 usually connects Botafogo and Urca.

Bus

Lines often used for Urca and Praia Vermelha include 107, 167, 513, 518 and 519.

Car

The park has no parking of its own and spaces in Urca are limited. There are car parks in Botafogo: Botafogo Praia Shopping, Shopping Rio Sul and Casa & Gourmet.

Bicycle

Praia Vermelha usually has bike-share stations. Bring a lock and use proper places outside the park access area.

On foot

From Shopping Rio Sul or Casa & Gourmet, the walk to Praia Vermelha is short to moderate. Consider heat, rain and the time you have.

Step by step

From the park gate to your table

  1. Get to Praia Vermelha

    Use "Avenida Pasteur, 520" or "Parque Bondinho Pão de Açúcar" in your app or GPS.

  2. Go up to Urca Hill

    By cable car, with a park ticket, or via the trail when open.

  3. Follow the signs

    At the first stop, follow the signs to Embaixada Carioca. Book ahead for weekends, holidays and groups.
