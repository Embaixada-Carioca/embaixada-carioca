Quick answer

What is Urca Hill?

Urca Hill (Morro da Urca) is the first stop of the Sugarloaf cable car, 220 meters above sea level, in the Urca neighborhood. From there, Sugarloaf Mountain stands right in front of you, with Guanabara Bay, the South Zone and downtown Rio around it.

Urca Hill has viewpoints, the end of the trail and Embaixada Carioca, a Brazilian restaurant open Monday to Friday from 8:45 AM to 8:45 PM and on weekends from 7:45 AM to 8:45 PM. Many visitors go up to Sugarloaf Mountain and come back to Urca Hill to eat.
