Quick answer

Where to eat at Sugarloaf Mountain?

Embaixada Carioca is a restaurant on Urca Hill, at the first stop of Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park. It serves breakfast until 11:30 AM, Brazilian lunch from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM and drinks with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

To go up by cable car, buy the park ticket separately. The restaurant booking secures your table, not access to the attraction.
