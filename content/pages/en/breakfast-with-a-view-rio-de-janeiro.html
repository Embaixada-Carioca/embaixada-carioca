Quick answer

Where to have breakfast with a view in Rio?

At Embaixada Carioca, on Urca Hill, inside the Sugarloaf cable-car park. Breakfast is served every day until 11:30 AM (opens 8:45 AM; 7:45 AM on weekends), à la carte, with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

It is an easy first stop: have breakfast on Urca Hill, then continue up to Sugarloaf Mountain with your park ticket.

How to plan your morning

  1. Arrive at Praia Vermelha

    The Bondinho Park entrance is at Av. Pasteur, 520, in Urca.

  2. Go up to Urca Hill

    By cable car, with a ticket, or by the trail, when open.

  3. Have breakfast, then continue

    Breakfast until 11:30 AM, then take the second leg up to Sugarloaf Mountain.
