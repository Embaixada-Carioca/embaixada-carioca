Where to watch the sunset on Urca Hill?

At Embaixada Carioca, on Urca Hill, the first cable-car stop. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain, open every day until 8:45 PM.

Arrive about 1 hour before sunset, pick a table with a view and order a caipirinha, a draft beer or snacks to share. Before going down, check the time of the last cable car.
