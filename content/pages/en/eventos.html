An event on Urca Hill

Embaixada Carioca hosts corporate events, groups and celebrations on Urca Hill, 220 meters above sea level, with views of Sugarloaf Mountain and Guanabara Bay.

The team combines a tourist location, Brazilian food and restaurant operations to build the right format, always respecting the flow of the park and the experience of other visitors.

How to request a proposal

The events form is available in Portuguese, English and Spanish. Once you submit it, your request goes to the team to prepare the proposal.

  1. Tell us about the group

    Date, time, number of guests, profile and purpose of the event.

  2. Choose the format

    Breakfast, lunch, welcome drink, cocktail party, workshop or tourism experience.

  3. Receive the proposal

    The team replies with the menu, space and service.
