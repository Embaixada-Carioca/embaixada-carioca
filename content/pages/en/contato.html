Which channel should I use?

For a table, Tagme or WhatsApp. For events, the form or the events email. For questions about your visit, use WhatsApp.

Embaixada Carioca is on Urca Hill, inside Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park, and is open Monday to Friday from 8:45 AM to 8:45 PM and on weekends from 7:45 AM to 8:45 PM.
