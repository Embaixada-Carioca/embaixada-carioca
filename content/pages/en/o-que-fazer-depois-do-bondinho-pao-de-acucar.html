Quick answer

Where to eat after the cable car?

No need to go down: Embaixada Carioca is at the first cable-car stop, on Urca Hill. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

After going up to the top, come back to Urca Hill for lunch, a draft beer or the sunset before ending your visit.

Step by step

How to fit it into your day

  1. Choose your access

    Cable car with a ticket, or the Urca Hill trail, when open.

  2. Choose the moment

    Breakfast, lunch, drinks or an event. Book for weekends, holidays or groups.

  3. Continue your visit

    After your meal, explore the Urca Hill viewpoints or take the next cable-car leg.
