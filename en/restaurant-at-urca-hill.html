Quick answer

Is there a restaurant on Urca Hill?

Yes: Embaixada Carioca, at the first cable-car stop, inside Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park. It is the only restaurant in the park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain, operating since 2010.

It serves à la carte breakfast, Brazilian lunch with the Academia da Cachaça feijoada (through a formal partnership), snacks, sandwiches, caipirinhas and Heineken draft beer, every day from 8:30 AM to 9:00 PM.

FAQ

Questions before your visit

Is Embaixada Carioca inside Bondinho Park?

Yes. It is at the top of Urca Hill, at the first stop of the Sugarloaf cable car, not down in the Urca neighborhood.

Do I need a cable-car ticket?

To go up by cable car, yes: the park ticket is bought separately. By the Urca Hill trail, when open, you can reach the restaurant without a cable-car ticket. Riding down by cable car is paid.

What are the hours for each service?

Breakfast from 8:30 to 11:30 AM, lunch and feijoada from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM, snacks and sandwiches from 11:30 AM to 8:30 PM. Drinks until 9:00 PM.

Does Embaixada Carioca have a view of Sugarloaf Mountain?

Yes. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain, right in front of you during your meal.

Where can I see the full menu with prices?

On the online menu, with updated prices and language options, or on the menu page.

How do I book a table?

Through Tagme or WhatsApp. A table reservation does not include the cable-car ticket.
