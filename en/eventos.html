Quick answer

An event on Urca Hill

Embaixada Carioca hosts corporate events, groups and celebrations on Urca Hill, 220 meters above sea level, with views of Sugarloaf Mountain and Guanabara Bay.

The team combines a tourist location, Brazilian food and restaurant operations to build the right format, always respecting the flow of the park and the experience of other visitors.

How to request a proposal

The events form is available in Portuguese and English. Once you submit it, your request goes to the team to prepare the proposal.

  1. Tell us about the group

    Date, time, number of guests, profile and purpose of the event.

  2. Choose the format

    Breakfast, lunch, welcome drink, cocktail party, workshop or tourism experience.

  3. Receive the proposal

    The team replies with the menu, space and service.

FAQ

Events questions

Does Embaixada Carioca host corporate events?

Yes. We host companies, agencies, groups, launches, workshops, meetings, lunches and relationship experiences.

Is the venue at the top of Sugarloaf Mountain?

No. Embaixada Carioca is on Urca Hill, at the first stop of the Sugarloaf cable car.

How do I request a proposal?

Use the events form, WhatsApp +55 21 96683-7556 or email eventos@embaixadacarioca.com.br, with the date, time, number of guests and preferred format.

Are there group menus?

Yes. The team prepares breakfast, lunch, cocktail, welcome drink and food experience options, including buffet formats for events.

What is the capacity?

It depends on the format, setup and areas used. Include the number of guests in your request to receive a suitable proposal.

Is the cable-car ticket included?

Access to Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park is handled separately. Discuss guest logistics with the team when you request a proposal.
