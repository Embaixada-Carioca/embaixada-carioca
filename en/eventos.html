03 Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about events at Urca Hill.

What is the maximum capacity of the venue?

Capacity at the Urca Hill venue varies according to the event format, layout, service plan and areas used. The panoramic terraces — with views of Sugarloaf Mountain — can be combined with the main hall for larger groups. Request a tailored proposal with your estimated guest count.

Do you work with travel agencies?

Yes. We welcome travel-agency groups for breakfast, award-winning feijoada from Academia da Cachaça lunches and sunset itineraries, with multilingual menus, a welcoming host and coordinated access.

How do guests access the venue?

The event venue on Urca Hill is accessed from Av. Pasteur, 520, usually by cable car through Sugarloaf Cable Car Park. The Praia Vermelha trail is an alternative when open. For events, our team coordinates guest access and arrival logistics in advance.

Which event formats do you host?

Executive meetings, corporate lunches, team dinners, product launches, press events, brand activations, training sessions, intimate weddings and private celebrations.

Are there time restrictions for events?

Events normally follow the cable car park's operating hours. Special evening extensions may be possible when arranged in advance with the park's operations team.

Can the menu be customised?

Yes. We offer set or customised menus, including vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options, plus adaptations for international groups.

Does Embaixada Carioca host corporate events?

Yes. The venue hosts corporate events, incentive groups, product launches, relationship lunches, workshops and experiences for tourism agencies and companies.

Does the event space have a Sugarloaf view?

Yes. The main strength of the venue is its location at Urca Hill, inside Sugarloaf Cable Car Park, with views of Sugarloaf Mountain and Rio.

Do guests need cable car tickets for events?

Access by cable car usually requires a Sugarloaf Cable Car Park ticket. For events, access conditions should be aligned during the proposal and operation planning.

What event formats are possible?

Possible formats include breakfast, coffee break, welcome drink, cocktail, lunch, workshop and Brazilian food experiences, depending on time, group size and structure.

How do I request a proposal?

Can gifts or trophies be presented during the event?

Yes, when aligned in advance. The team can plan a support table, service flow and timing for the moment.

What makes the venue different?

The key difference is the combination of iconic view, Brazilian gastronomy, location inside Sugarloaf Cable Car Park and a truly Rio-style experience.
