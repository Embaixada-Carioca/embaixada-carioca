FAQ

Questions about contact and reservations

How do I book a table?

Through Tagme or WhatsApp +55 21 96683-7556. Include the date, time and number of people.

Does the reservation include the cable-car ticket?

No. The Bondinho Park ticket is bought separately, on the official website or at the ticket office.

How do I request an event proposal?

Through the events form or by email at eventos@embaixadacarioca.com.br, with the date, time, number of people and format.

How do I contact you about privacy and personal data?

By email at financeiro@embaixadacarioca.com.br, with the subject “Privacidade e dados pessoais”. See the privacy policy (in Portuguese).
