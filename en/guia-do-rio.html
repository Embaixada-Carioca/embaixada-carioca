Quick answer

Where to start?

At Praia Vermelha, in Urca, where the Bondinho Park entrance is. From there, the cable car goes to Urca Hill and then to the top of Sugarloaf Mountain.

Embaixada Carioca is on Urca Hill, operating since 2010, with breakfast, Brazilian lunch and drinks facing Sugarloaf Mountain.

FAQ

Questions about the area

Where to eat during a Sugarloaf visit?

On Urca Hill, at Embaixada Carioca, the only restaurant in Bondinho Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

What is the difference between Urca Hill and Sugarloaf Mountain?

Urca Hill, 220 meters high, is the first cable-car stop. Sugarloaf Mountain, 396 meters high, is the second. Embaixada Carioca is on Urca Hill.

How much time should I allow?

4 to 5 hours to visit both hills at an easy pace, including a meal and time for photos.

Where can I check cable-car hours and tickets?

On the official Bondinho Park website. A table reservation at Embaixada Carioca does not include the ticket.
