Half-day Urca and Sugarloaf itinerary

Praia Vermelha, the cable car, the top of Sugarloaf Mountain and a meal with a view on Urca Hill, in half a day and without rushing.

Quick answer

How to do Urca and Sugarloaf in half a day?

Start at Praia Vermelha, go up to Urca Hill, continue to the top of Sugarloaf Mountain and come back to Urca Hill to eat. At an easy pace, the itinerary takes 4 to 5 hours.

Embaixada Carioca is at the first cable-car stop and serves breakfast from 8:30 to 11:30 AM, lunch from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM and drinks until 9:00 PM.

Step by step

The itinerary in six stops

  1. Praia Vermelha

    Start at the foot of Sugarloaf Mountain, in front of the Bondinho Park entrance at Av. Pasteur, 520.

  2. Up to Urca Hill

    By cable car, with a park ticket, or by the Urca Hill trail, when open.

  3. Breakfast or lunch at Embaixada Carioca

    Breakfast until 11:30 AM or Brazilian lunch from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM, facing Sugarloaf Mountain.

  4. Top of Sugarloaf Mountain

    Take the second cable-car leg up to Sugarloaf Mountain, 396 meters high.

  5. Back to Urca Hill

    On the way down, stop on Urca Hill again for the viewpoints and a break.

  6. Sunset or ride down

    A caipirinha or a draft beer in the late afternoon before heading down. Check the last cable car of the day.

Where to eat

The meal of the itinerary

Embaixada Carioca is the only restaurant in Bondinho Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain, rated 4.8 on Google. On the menu: Brazilian-style picanha, the Academia da Cachaça feijoada (awarded by Veja Rio Comer & Beber 2025/2026 and served through a formal partnership) and Heineken draft beer.

Park hours and the last cable car: check the official Bondinho website.

FAQ

Questions about the itinerary

Is Embaixada Carioca worth including in a Sugarloaf itinerary?

Yes. It is on Urca Hill, at the first cable-car stop, and makes a natural break for breakfast, lunch, caipirinhas or sunset with a view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

How much time should I allow?

4 to 5 hours, including the meal, the top of Sugarloaf Mountain and time for photos.

What is the best time to start?

Around 9:00 AM, to enjoy the morning and have breakfast until 11:30 AM, or around 3:00 PM, to end with the sunset on Urca Hill.

Do I need a ticket?

By cable car, yes: the park ticket is bought separately, on the official website. The Urca Hill trail is free when it is open. A table reservation does not include the ticket.

Should groups book?

Yes. Booking is recommended for groups, weekends, holidays and busy days.
