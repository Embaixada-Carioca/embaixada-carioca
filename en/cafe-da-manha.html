Quick answer

What is breakfast like on Urca Hill?

Embaixada Carioca serves breakfast every day from 8:30 to 11:30 AM on Urca Hill, 220 meters above sea level. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain, and you enjoy the view without going up to the summit.

Breakfast is à la carte. To share, the Embaixada breakfast for 2 brings sourdough breads, cold cuts, yogurt, granola, fruit, cake, mini açaí and cheese bread. There are also omelettes, tapiocas, griddled sandwiches, açaí, specialty coffee and fresh juices.

On your visit

How to fit breakfast into your visit

Breakfast pairs well with the first cable car of the day, when morning light falls on Guanabara Bay.

1. Arrival

Enter the park early

The entrance is at Av. Pasteur, 520, Praia Vermelha. By cable car, buy the park ticket; your table booking is separate.

Official tickets →

2. The ride

Get off at Urca Hill

The first cable-car leg ends on Urca Hill, where the restaurant is. Via the trail, when open, the climb starts at Pista Cláudio Coutinho.

Getting there →

3. After breakfast

Keep exploring

Enjoy the Urca Hill viewpoints and continue to Sugarloaf Mountain. You can come back for lunch from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM.

FAQ

Breakfast questions

What time is breakfast served?

Every day from 8:30 to 11:30 AM.

Where is the breakfast?

On Urca Hill (Morro da Urca), inside Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park, at the first cable-car stop. The park entrance is at Av. Pasteur, 520, Urca, Rio de Janeiro.

Do I need a cable-car ticket?

By cable car, yes: the park ticket is sold separately. Via the Urca Hill trail, when open, you can reach the restaurant without a cable-car ticket. Your meal is paid separately.

Is breakfast a buffet?

No. Breakfast is à la carte: there is the Embaixada breakfast for 2 and individual dishes such as omelettes, tapiocas, sandwiches, açaí, coffee and fresh juices.

Where can I see the menu with prices?

In the online menu, with current prices and several languages. A selection is on this page and on the menu page.

Should I book?

Yes, especially on weekends, holidays and for groups. Book on Tagme or via WhatsApp +55 21 96683-7556.

Does breakfast have a view of Sugarloaf Mountain?

Yes. Embaixada Carioca is on Urca Hill and is the only restaurant in Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

Can I continue the tour after breakfast?

Yes. Enjoy the Urca Hill viewpoints and continue by cable car to Sugarloaf Mountain with your park ticket. If you came up the trail, plan your way down before access closes.
