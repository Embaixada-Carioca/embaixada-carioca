Quick answer

What is Urca Hill?

Urca Hill (Morro da Urca) is the first stop of the Sugarloaf cable car, 220 meters above sea level, in the Urca neighborhood. From there, Sugarloaf Mountain stands right in front of you, with Guanabara Bay, the South Zone and downtown Rio around it.

Urca Hill has viewpoints, the end of the trail and Embaixada Carioca, a Brazilian restaurant open Monday to Friday from 8:45 AM to 8:45 PM and on weekends from 7:45 AM to 8:45 PM. Many visitors go up to Sugarloaf Mountain and come back to Urca Hill to eat.

FAQ

Questions about Urca Hill

How high is Urca Hill?

220 meters. It is the first cable-car stop, before Sugarloaf Mountain, which is 396 meters high.

Is there a restaurant on Urca Hill?

Yes. Embaixada Carioca is open Monday to Friday from 8:45 AM to 8:45 PM and on weekends from 7:45 AM to 8:45 PM, with breakfast until 11:30 AM, lunch from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM and drinks in the late afternoon. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

How do I get to Urca Hill?

Through Bondinho Park, at Av. Pasteur, 520, Urca: the first cable-car leg leaves from Praia Vermelha. The alternative is the Urca Hill trail, from Pista Cláudio Coutinho, when it is open. See how to get there.

What is the difference between Urca Hill and Sugarloaf Mountain?

Urca Hill (220 m) is the first stop, with the restaurant, viewpoints and trail access. Sugarloaf Mountain (396 m) is the higher peak, reached by the second cable-car leg. From Urca Hill, Sugarloaf Mountain stands right in front of you.

Do I need a ticket for Urca Hill?

By cable car, yes. If you take the trail when it is open and stay on Urca Hill, you do not need a cable-car ticket. You need one to continue to Sugarloaf Mountain or to ride down.

Is Urca Hill open at night?

Embaixada Carioca is open until 8:45 PM every day. Bondinho Park hours (October 2026): Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 9:00 PM; Saturday and Sunday, 7:30 AM to 9:00 PM; last boarding at 7:30 PM. Check holidays and events on the official Bondinho website.
