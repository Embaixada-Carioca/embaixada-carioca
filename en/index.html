Our story

Opened in 2020 to welcome
the world high above Rio — Restaurant Urca Hill.

The name carries more than a century of affectionate memory: in the 1920s to 1950s, the Rio press called "embaixada carioca" every delegation of notables that left Rio to represent the city to the world. Today, the Restaurant Urca Hill reverses this logic: the world comes to Rio — and Embaixada is up there to welcome it.

Among restaurants with a view in Rio de Janeiro, Embaixada Carioca holds a unique position: the only restaurant with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain, at the cable car's first stop, 220 metres above sea level. Architecture by Engelhaus Arquitetura and visual identity by Refinaria Design — with floor-to-ceiling windows facing Sugarloaf Mountain and Guanabara Bay. The house mission: to be the consulate of Brazilian gastronomy and culture for the world.

Does the restaurant have a view of Sugarloaf or Sugarloaf Mountain?

Is the Sugarloaf cable car ticket included in the restaurant reservation?

No. The Sugarloaf Mountain cable car ticket (Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park) must be purchased separately at bondinho.com.br. The Embaixada Carioca reservation is exclusively for your table at the restaurant. Tip: buy your cable car ticket in advance online to avoid queues.

Is the restaurant at the first cable car stop or at Urca Hill, the first Sugarloaf Cable Car stop?

Embaixada Carioca is located at the first cable car stop — Urca Hill, at 220 meters altitude. You do not need to go up to Sugarloaf Mountain (second stop, 396 meters) to reach the restaurant. When you board the cable car at Praia Vermelha, simply get off at the first station — the restaurant is just a few steps from the cable car exit.

Can I reach the restaurant by hiking trail without paying for the cable car?

Yes! The Praia Vermelha hiking trail is a free alternative to reach Urca Hill. The trail opens at 8:30 AM (Fridays to Sundays) and takes about 30 minutes. In heavy rain, the trail may be closed for safety. Those who hike up do not need to pay for the cable car to access the restaurant.

I have a 9:30 AM reservation — should I worry about cable car queues?

We recommend arriving at Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park 30 to 45 minutes before your reservation time, especially on weekends and holidays. The cable car operates from 8:30 AM (Fri–Sun) and 9:00 AM (Mon–Thu). If you are running late, please contact us on WhatsApp +55 21 96683-7556 — we will do our best to accommodate you.
