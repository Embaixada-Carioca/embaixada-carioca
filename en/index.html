Quick answer

Where to eat at Sugarloaf Mountain?

Embaixada Carioca is a restaurant on Urca Hill, at the first stop of Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park. It serves breakfast until 11:30 AM, Brazilian lunch from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM and drinks with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

To go up by cable car, buy the park ticket separately. The restaurant booking secures your table, not access to the attraction.

FAQ

The essentials, no surprises.

Clear information to plan your route and your booking.

Where is Embaixada Carioca?

On Urca Hill (Morro da Urca), the first cable-car stop, inside Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park. The main park entrance is at Av. Pasteur, 520, Urca.

Does the reservation include the cable-car ticket?

No. The reservation is for your table at the restaurant. The ticket and access to the park are purchased separately.

What are breakfast and lunch hours?

Breakfast is served until 11:30 AM. Lunch runs from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM. The restaurant is open daily from 8:30 AM to 9:00 PM.

Do I need to book?

Booking is recommended, especially on weekends, holidays and for groups. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability.

Is the restaurant at the top of Sugarloaf Mountain?

No. Embaixada Carioca is on Urca Hill, the first stop of the cable car, and faces Sugarloaf Mountain. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

Is feijoada served every day?

Yes, at lunch from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM. It is Academia da Cachaça's feijoada, winner of Best Feijoada at Veja Rio Comer & Beber 2025/2026, served through a formal partnership.
