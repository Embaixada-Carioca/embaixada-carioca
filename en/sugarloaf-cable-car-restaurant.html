Quick answer

Is there a restaurant on the Sugarloaf cable car?

Yes. Embaixada Carioca is at the first cable-car stop, on Urca Hill, before the leg up to Sugarloaf Mountain. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

It serves breakfast, Brazilian lunch with the Academia da Cachaça feijoada (through a formal partnership), caipirinhas and Heineken draft beer. The restaurant reservation and the park ticket are separate.

FAQ

Questions about the cable-car restaurant

Is there a restaurant on the Sugarloaf cable car?

Yes. Embaixada Carioca is on Urca Hill, the first cable-car station, with tables indoors and on the terrace facing Sugarloaf Mountain.

Do I need a cable-car ticket?

To go up by cable car, yes. The park ticket is bought on the official website or at the ticket office, separately from the table reservation. By the Urca Hill trail, when open, you can get there without a ticket.

Does it serve breakfast and lunch?

Yes. Breakfast from 8:30 to 11:30 AM, lunch from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM, and snacks, sandwiches and drinks until the late afternoon.

Can I eat at the top of Sugarloaf Mountain?

For a full meal with a view of Sugarloaf Mountain, Urca Hill is the place to stop. Many visitors go up to the top and come back to Urca Hill to eat.
