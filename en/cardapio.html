What is on the menu?

Brazilian food from breakfast to sunset, on Urca Hill. Highlights are Brazilian-style picanha, Academia da Cachaça's feijoada (served through a formal partnership), snacks to share, caipirinhas and Heineken draft beer, from the venue that placed 2nd in Heineken Masters 2025.

FAQ

Menu questions

Is feijoada served every day?

Yes. Academia da Cachaça's feijoada, winner of Best Feijoada at Veja Rio Comer & Beber 2025/2026, is served at Embaixada Carioca through a formal partnership, every day from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM. It serves 2 and costs R$ 189.70.

What time is breakfast?

Every day from 8:30 to 11:30 AM. Breakfast is à la carte, with the Embaixada breakfast for 2 and individual dishes.

Where can I see all the prices?

In the official online menu, with current prices and several languages. This page shows a selection checked on 19 October 2026.

Do you take reservations?

Yes. Book on Tagme or via WhatsApp +55 21 96683-7556, especially on weekends and holidays.

Are there vegetarian options?

Yes, among starters, salads and sides. For specific dietary needs, contact the team on WhatsApp before your visit.

Does the reservation include the cable-car ticket?

No. The reservation is for your table. The Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park ticket is bought separately, online or at the ticket office.
