Direct answer

How do I fit the restaurant into my tour?

Embaixada Carioca is on Morro da Urca, the cable car's first stop, inside Parque Bondinho Pão de Açúcar. Buy a park ticket to ride the cable car and make a separate restaurant reservation to secure a table.

Have breakfast until 11:30 am, Brazilian lunch from 11:30 am to 5 pm (6 pm on weekends), or drinks in the late afternoon. Check park operations on its official channel on the day of your visit.

Checklist

Plan with three decisions.

  1. Choose your time

    Breakfast until 11:30 am, lunch from 11:30 am to 5 pm (6 pm on weekends), or late afternoon. Allow extra time on weekends and holidays.

  2. Choose how to go up

    The cable car is the standard access and requires a ticket. The trail is a free alternative when open; check conditions and plan your way down.

  3. Book separately

    The park ticket covers the cable car. The Tagme reservation is for your restaurant table only. Neither includes the other.

Arrival

How to reach Morro da Urca.

By cable car

Go to Parque Bondinho at Av. Pasteur, 520, Urca. The first cable car section ends on Morro da Urca, where the restaurant is located.

Official hours and tickets

By trail

The Morro da Urca trail starts at Pista Cláudio Coutinho and may provide free access when open. Check official conditions and plan your descent.

Detailed access guide

Taxi or ride app

Search for “Parque Bondinho Pão de Açúcar” or “Av. Pasteur, 520”. Drop-off is at the park entrance; the restaurant is uphill on Morro da Urca.

Public transportation

Botafogo metro station is a common connection for a bus or ride app to Praia Vermelha. Confirm the live route on the day.

Frequently asked questions

Answers for your itinerary.

Do I need a ticket to visit Embaixada Carioca?

Yes, if you ride the cable car: purchase a Parque Bondinho ticket separately. The trail may provide free access when it is open. Check conditions before leaving.

Does the restaurant reservation include the cable car?

No. It secures your restaurant table; park admission and access are separate.

Where can I have breakfast with a view in Rio?

Embaixada Carioca serves breakfast until 11:30 am on Morro da Urca with a direct Sugarloaf Mountain view.

Where can I have lunch on Morro da Urca?

At Embaixada Carioca, inside Parque Bondinho. Brazilian lunch is served from 11:30 am to 5 pm (6 pm on weekends).

What address should I use in GPS?

Use Parque Bondinho Pão de Açúcar or Av. Pasteur, 520, Urca. This is the park entrance; the restaurant is on Morro da Urca.

How much time should I allow?

Allow time for arrival, ascent, sightseeing and your meal. Weekends, holidays and sunset periods may need extra margin.
