For a meal during your Sugarloaf visit, Embaixada Carioca is on Urca Hill at the first cable-car stop. Choose breakfast until 11:30 AM or Brazilian lunch from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM.

Quick answer

Where to eat at Sugarloaf Mountain?

On Urca Hill, the first cable-car stop, at Embaixada Carioca. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain, open every day from 8:30 AM to 9:00 PM and operating since 2010.

Pick your moment: breakfast until 11:30 AM, Brazilian lunch from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM, or drinks and snacks in the late afternoon. A table reservation does not include the park ticket.

In the area

Inside the park or in Urca?

Embaixada Carioca is inside Bondinho Park, on Urca Hill. It is the option for eating during your visit without going back down.

At Embaixada Carioca, your table faces Sugarloaf Mountain. Outside the park, the Urca neighborhood has street-level restaurants for before or after your visit.

FAQ

Questions about where to eat

Is Embaixada Carioca inside Bondinho Park?

Yes. It is on Urca Hill, at the first cable-car stop, not at street level.

Do I need a cable-car ticket to eat there?

By cable car, yes: the park ticket is bought separately. By the Urca Hill trail, when open, you can get there without a cable-car ticket.

Is it better to eat on Urca Hill or at the top of Sugarloaf Mountain?

For a full meal with a view of Sugarloaf Mountain, Urca Hill is the place: Embaixada Carioca serves breakfast, lunch and drinks there. Many visitors go up to the top and come back to Urca Hill to eat.

Can I eat on Urca Hill without a reservation?

Yes, depending on table availability. For weekends, holidays, special dates and groups, book through Tagme or WhatsApp.
