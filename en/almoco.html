Quick answer

Where to have lunch on Urca Hill?

Embaixada Carioca serves Brazilian lunch every day from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM on Urca Hill, the first cable-car stop. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

The menu features classic Brazilian recipes: Brazilian-style picanha, Carioca filet mignon picadinho, shrimp bobó, salmon with passion fruit sauce and Academia da Cachaça's feijoada, served through a formal partnership.

FAQ

Lunch questions

Is lunch served every day?

Yes, every day from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Snacks and sandwiches are served until 8:30 PM.

What is the most popular dish?

Brazilian-style picanha, served individually (about 200 g) or for 2 (about 400 g). Next comes Academia da Cachaça's feijoada, served for 2.

Is feijoada served at lunch?

Yes, every day from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM. It is Academia da Cachaça's feijoada, winner of Best Feijoada at Veja Rio Comer & Beber 2025/2026, served at Embaixada Carioca through a formal partnership.

How do I book a table for lunch?

On Tagme or via WhatsApp +55 21 96683-7556. We recommend booking for weekends, holidays and groups of more than 6.

Do I need a cable-car ticket to have lunch?

By cable car, yes: the park ticket is sold separately. Via the Urca Hill trail, when open, you can arrive without a cable-car ticket.

Can you host group or company lunches?

Yes. For groups and events, the team prepares lunch proposals and other formats. See the events page.
