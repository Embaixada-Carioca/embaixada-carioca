Quick answer

Why do guests choose Embaixada Carioca?

For the direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain, the location inside Bondinho Park and the Brazilian food. The restaurant is rated 4.8 on Google.

It is a practical stop for breakfast, lunch, caipirinhas and snacks during the visit, without leaving Urca Hill.
