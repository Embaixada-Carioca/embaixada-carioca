Quick answer

How does the Sugarloaf cable car work?

Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park connects Praia Vermelha to Urca Hill (220 m) and Sugarloaf Mountain (396 m) in two cable-car legs. The entrance is at Av. Pasteur, 520, in Urca, and tickets are sold on the official website and at the ticket offices.

At the first stop, Embaixada Carioca serves breakfast, lunch and drinks with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain. A restaurant reservation is separate from the park ticket.

The park

Much more than a cable car

Opened in 1912, the Sugarloaf cable car was the first in Brazil. Today, the cabins take visitors from Praia Vermelha to Urca Hill and, from there, to the top of Sugarloaf Mountain.

Urca Hill has viewpoints, the end of the trail and Embaixada Carioca. Sugarloaf Mountain offers a 360-degree panorama of Rio. Allow 3 to 5 hours for the full visit with a meal.

FAQ

Questions about the Sugarloaf cable car

What are the Bondinho Park hours?

Park hours and the last cable car vary: check the official Bondinho website. Embaixada Carioca is open every day from 8:30 AM to 9:00 PM.

Is there a restaurant in Bondinho Park?

Yes. Embaixada Carioca is on Urca Hill, the first stop, and is the only restaurant in Bondinho Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain. It serves breakfast, lunch with feijoada and drinks.

How do I buy a cable-car ticket?

On the official website or at the park ticket offices. Buying online in advance is recommended, especially on weekends and holidays.

Do I need a cable-car ticket to visit Embaixada Carioca?

By cable car, yes. If you take the Urca Hill trail when it is open and stay on Urca Hill, you do not need a ticket. You need one to continue to Sugarloaf Mountain or to ride down. A restaurant reservation does not include the ticket.

What else is there to do in Bondinho Park?

Enjoy the viewpoints on Urca Hill and Sugarloaf Mountain, hike the Urca Hill trail and stop at Embaixada Carioca for breakfast, lunch or sunset.
