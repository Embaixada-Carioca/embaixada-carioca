Quick answer

Where to have breakfast with a view in Rio?

At Embaixada Carioca, on Urca Hill, inside the Sugarloaf cable-car park. Breakfast is served every day from 8:30 to 11:30 AM, à la carte, with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

It is an easy first stop: have breakfast on Urca Hill, then continue up to Sugarloaf Mountain with your park ticket.

FAQ

Questions about breakfast

Where can I have breakfast with a view in Rio?

At Embaixada Carioca, on Urca Hill, the first stop of the Sugarloaf cable car. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

What time is breakfast served?

Every day from 8:30 to 11:30 AM, à la carte.

Is it a buffet?

No. Breakfast is à la carte: the Embaixada breakfast for 2, omelets, tapiocas, açaí, sandwiches and specialty coffee.

Do I need a cable-car ticket?

By cable car, yes: the park ticket is bought separately. By the Urca Hill trail, when open, you can get there without a ticket.
