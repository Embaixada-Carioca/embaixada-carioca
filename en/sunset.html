Quick answer

Where to watch the sunset on Urca Hill?

At Embaixada Carioca, on Urca Hill, the first cable-car stop. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain, open every day until 9:00 PM.

Arrive about 1 hour before sunset, pick a table with a view and order a caipirinha, a draft beer or snacks to share. Before going down, check the time of the last cable car.

FAQ

Sunset questions

What time does Embaixada Carioca close?

It is open every day from 8:30 AM to 9:00 PM. Snacks and sandwiches are served until 8:30 PM.

When should I arrive to see the sunset?

About 1 hour before the day's sunset time, to watch the light change from blue sky to warm colours.

Is there live music?

On some days there is live music or a DJ. The schedule changes: check Instagram or ask the team before going up.

Do I need a cable-car ticket?

By cable car, yes: the park ticket is sold separately. Via the trail, when open, you can arrive without a cable-car ticket, but plan your way down before access closes.

How do I get back after sunset?

Check the last cable car down on the official Bondinho website on the day of your visit.

Can I book for a birthday or a group?

Yes. Book on Tagme or WhatsApp. For larger celebrations, see the events page.
