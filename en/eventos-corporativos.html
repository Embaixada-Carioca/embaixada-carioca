A company event at Sugarloaf Mountain

Embaixada Carioca hosts corporate events on Urca Hill, 220 meters above sea level, with a view of Sugarloaf Mountain and Guanabara Bay.

From an executive breakfast to sunset cocktails, the team builds the format with Brazilian food and table service, respecting Bondinho Park's flow.

FAQ

Questions about corporate events

What kinds of corporate events do you host?

Meetings, workshops, training sessions, launches, client relationship events, incentive lunches, team celebrations and cocktails.

How does guest access work?

Access to Bondinho Park is handled separately. Agree on guest logistics with the team when you request the proposal.

Can the menu be customized?

Yes. The team puts together breakfast, lunch, cocktail, welcome drink and buffet options to suit the event. Include any dietary restrictions in your request.

What are the event hours?

Events follow Bondinho Park's operating hours. Extended hours depend on prior agreement.

What is the capacity?

It depends on the format, the setup and the areas used. Include the number of people in your request to get the right proposal.
