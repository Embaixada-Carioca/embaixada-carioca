What to do after the Sugarloaf cable car

Quick answer

Where to eat after the cable car?

No need to go down: Embaixada Carioca is at the first cable-car stop, on Urca Hill. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

After going up to the top, come back to Urca Hill for lunch, a draft beer or the sunset before ending your visit.

FAQ

Questions before your visit

Is Embaixada Carioca inside Bondinho Park?

Yes. It is on Urca Hill, the first stop of Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park.

Do I need to plan access before I go?

Yes. Most visitors ride the cable car with a park ticket. The Urca Hill trail is a free alternative when it is open.

Is it better for breakfast, lunch or drinks?

All three work: breakfast to start early, lunch for Brazilian food and the late afternoon for caipirinhas, draft beer and the sunset.

Should groups book?

Yes. Booking is recommended for groups, weekends, holidays and busy days.

Can I go with children?

Yes. The menu has a kids' menu (up to 10 years old), sandwiches and dishes such as picadinho.
