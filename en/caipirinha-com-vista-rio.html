Quick answer

Where to have a caipirinha with a view in Rio?

On the Embaixada Carioca terrace, on Urca Hill, the first cable-car stop. It is the only restaurant in Bondinho Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

The house caipirinha is made with aged cachaça, ginger, herbal bitters and limes. To go with it: codfish fritters, pastéis or fried cassava.

FAQ

Questions about the drinks

What is in the house caipirinha?

Aged cachaça, ginger, herbal bitters and Sicilian and Tahiti limes. It costs R$ 42.00, according to the online menu checked on 19 October 2026.

What other drinks are on the menu?

Mojito with Havana Club 3 años, Aperol Spritz, Heineken draft in a tulip glass or a mug, Heineken 0.0% and coconut water. See every option on the online menu.

Can I go just for drinks?

Yes. The terrace works for a late afternoon with only drinks and snacks, after your visit.

Do I need a cable-car ticket?

By cable car, yes: the park ticket is bought separately. By the Urca Hill trail, when open, you can get there without a ticket.

Do I need to book?

It is not required, but it is recommended for the late afternoon, weekends, holidays and groups.
